# animepoodle
A joyful anime, manga, manhwa, manhua and donghua discovery app for curious fans. Product interface, not marketing landing page. Inspired by the library Cinematic Style prompt's dark immersive imagery and subtle borders, adapted to an approachable editorial discovery experience.
## Tokens
Background #101219; sidebar #14161f; surface #1b1e29; elevated #222632; border #2b2e3b; primary text #f4f3f6; secondary #9698ab; coral accent #ff816f; lavender #b6a2ec; mint #98d8bd. Font: system sans-serif. Headings heavy, tight tracking, sentence case. Radius 12px for cards, 8px buttons, pill tags. Spacing multiples of 4 and 8. Gentle 180ms hover transitions, reduced-motion respected. No neon or excessive gradients.
## Layout
Desktop fixed 220px sidebar, compact topbar, content max-width 1440. Hero landscape anime artwork taking two-thirds with dark overlay, large title 'Your next obsession starts here.' and coral CTA; right third lavender 'A little you. A little magic.' recommendation taste card. Format pills. Trending cover grid six columns. Personal recommendation shelf and mood selector. On mobile collapse sidebar and use two-column card grid.
## Behavior
Search, format filters, detail dialog, saved library, taste sliders, mood controls, surprise me, informative match scores. Accessible keyboard controls, visible focus and touch targets. Quiet empty states. Taste profiles and library persist locally. Rust/WebAssembly mathematics runs client-side.
